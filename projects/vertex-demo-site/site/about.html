About Vertex

A shop floor that answers to a drawing.

Vertex started in 1968 as a four-machine job shop and still runs like one: every part traces to a person, a machine and a measurement.

How we work

We publish our tolerances, we measure every first article, and we tell customers when a run is at risk before they have to ask. It is less glamorous than a slogan, and it is why customers stay for decades.

Two plants, one standard. Whichever floor a part is cut on, it is held to the same drawing, the same gauge and the same sign-off.
