How a call works

01She asks what you need Robin answers and asks what the caller needs before asking who they are. A name spoken aloud is a greeting, not an identity.
02She proves who is calling Member ID and date of birth, checked against the plan record. She names the plan back and waits for a yes, and never asks for an SSN, user ID, password or PIN.
03She answers, or looks it up What the caller said routes to one of four procedures. Plan rules come from the published documents; anything specific to this caller triggers a live account lookup first.
04She connects, then records Past what she can do, she offers to put the caller through rather than read out a number. When the call ends, its transcript, topic, outcome and verification write themselves to the record.

How Robin is set up

Context-aware speech
eleven_v3_conversational with expressive delivery on. She reads a line the way its meaning calls for rather than at one flat pitch: a dollar figure slows, a question lifts. A pronunciation dictionary fixes plan terms a generic voice mangles.
Turn-taking and interruptions
Turn model turn_v3 with speculative turns, so she starts replying while the caller is still finishing, and makes a short thinking sound past three seconds rather than leave dead air. Interruptions are free, except during an identity check or a balance lookup.
Reasoning
claude-haiku-4-5 at a low temperature, with a ten-minute cap on any one call.
Knowledge
Six plan documents: five searched per question, the portal guide always in context. She answers from those only, and where they are silent says she is not certain and offers a transfer.
Account data
Two lookups against the plan record: identity, and the caller's own figures. No database sits in the call path, so a published answer adds no call time and an outage in the portal cannot reach a live call.
Routing
Four procedures: account recovery, plan questions, loans, the online portal. Each is triggered by what the caller says and bound to the documents it may use.
Language
English, and Spanish with its own greeting, switched automatically when the caller switches.
Guardrails
Topic-focus and prompt-injection protection on, content filters set to end a call that goes badly off the rails. Every call is recorded, transcribed and classified.

What she does, and what you can see

On the call

  • Verifies the caller from member ID and date of birth, before any answer, document, or confirmation that an account exists.
  • Answers plan questions on loans, rollovers, leaving the employer, contributions, the match and vesting, from the published documents.
  • Reads their own figures: balance, vested balance, and their loan down to the payment, rate, payments left and payoff date.
  • Enforces the one-loan rule by checking the caller's record first, so a general rule is never delivered as though it were theirs.
  • Offers to connect them to a specialist instead of handing out a number, giving the number only if they would rather call themselves.
  • Coaches a locked account or password reset without touching an SSN, user ID, password or PIN.
  • Asks four survey questions at the end of eligible calls, and stays silent where asking would be wrong.

Around the call, behind one sign-in

  • Quality. Whether callers would rather use Robin than hold for a person, how likely they are to recommend her, how natural she sounded, and their own words. Every figure opens the call behind it, with CSV export.
  • Accuracy. Each call graded against the documents she actually read on it, claim by claim, with the sentence backing each one. No answer key: what she said is checked against what she was given.
  • Calls. Every recent call: who verified, what they asked, how it ended, why it transferred, the sentiment, and the transcript with caller details scrubbed.
  • Knowledge Factory. Turn a plan document into answer cards, have a second model check each claim against its source, then publish. Cards holding a name, SSN or account number cannot be saved, and nothing publishes without a person.
  • Question Tester. Ask a question the way a caller would and see what her published knowledge answers, with no phone call.
  • About Robin. Her configuration read live from the platform on every page load, never from a document that may have drifted.

What this buys the plan

A gate that cannot be talked past
Identity is proven before any answer, including the general questions people assume are harmless. Pressure to skip it is refused, not negotiated.
Answers you can trace
Every plan answer traces to a named document and to the sentence that supports it. A wrong answer is findable, not anecdotal.
A limit becomes a handoff
What she cannot do ends in a warm transfer rather than homework. The caller moves forward on the call they are already on.
Changing her knowledge is a publish
New or corrected content is written, checked, approved by a person and published. Not a code change, and it leaves a record.
