Robin

Your NestEgg U Retirement Guide

Robin answers the retirement plan's phone line. She picks up on the first ring, at any hour, in English or Spanish. The same approach works on other phone lines too.

Hi! I'm Robin. Ask me anything about your 401(k).

What Robin is, and what she can do

Robin is a voice assistant that answers questions about contributions, loans, withdrawals, rollovers, leaving the company, and getting into the online account. She speaks English and Spanish. When a question needs human judgment, she hands the call to a person.

How she works

  1. She picks up. Robin answers on the first ring, at any hour. She can handle many callers at the same time.
  2. She makes sure it is you. Robin asks for two pieces of identification and checks them against the plan's records. She shares nothing about the account until that check passes.
  3. She looks it up. Robin answers plan questions from the approved documents, and gets personal figures from a live account lookup. She does not guess. When a document does not cover the question, she says so and offers to connect the caller to a person.
  4. She writes it down. After every call, Robin records why the caller phoned, what happened, and how it went. The team reviews that record instead of rebuilding it.

What she solves

The same question, all day

A short list of questions makes up most of a team's call volume. Robin answers all of them.

The hours

Questions arrive outside business hours. Robin answers them at ten on a Sunday night.

A second language

Staffing a second language on every shift is expensive. Robin speaks Spanish on every call.

The ramp-up

A new hire needs weeks of training before taking calls. Robin uses a new document the day it is published.

Where else this fits

Nothing about how Robin works is specific to retirement. She fits any phone line that meets four conditions.

  • The same questions arrive over and over.
  • The answers already live in documents that someone keeps current.
  • You need to know who is calling before you say anything personal.
  • There is a clear point where a person has to take over.
