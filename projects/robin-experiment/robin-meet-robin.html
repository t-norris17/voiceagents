She answers the retirement plan's phone line. First ring, any hour, English or Spanish.

Hi! I'm Robin. Ask me anything about your 401(k).

What Robin is

A voice assistant, not a phone tree. She confirms who is calling before anything personal is said out loud, answers plan questions from documents the plan has approved, looks up a verified caller's own figures, and puts someone through to a person the moment a question needs judgment. The fortieth caller of the morning gets the same answer as the first.

What she can do

Answer plan questions

Contributions, loans, withdrawals, rollovers, leaving, account access — all from approved documents.

Confirm who is calling

Member ID and date of birth against the plan's records. Nothing about the account until that clears.

Read a caller their own numbers

Balance, vested balance, contribution rate, loan status — looked up live, for the verified caller only.

Send a password reset

To the address already on file, while the caller is on the line, then she walks them through signing in.

Speak English and Spanish

She hears the switch and follows it, mid-call. No second number, no separate queue.

Hand off to a person

A warm transfer carrying the reason, so the caller does not start over and the specialist is not blind.

How she works

  1. She picks up. On the first ring, at any hour, for however many people are calling at once.
  2. She makes sure it is you. Two pieces of identification against the plan's records before a single figure is said out loud.
  3. She looks it up. Plan questions come from the approved documents, personal figures from a live lookup. She cannot fill a gap with a guess — when the answer is not there she says so and offers a person.
  4. She writes it down. Why they called, what happened, how it went — every call, automatically, so the team reviews the work instead of reconstructing it.

What she solves

The hold queue

Enrollment season, year end, a bad week in the market. Robin takes those calls at the same time, not in order.

The hours

Retirement questions turn up at ten on a Sunday night, which is when nobody is staffed to answer them.

Bilingual coverage

Staffing Spanish on every shift is expensive and fragile. Robin speaks it on every call.

The ramp-up

A new hire needs weeks on a plan before taking calls. Robin has the current document the day it is published.

Every routine call Robin takes is one the team does not. The calls that genuinely need a person still get one, faster, with the context already gathered.
