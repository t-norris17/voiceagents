Keeping Robin right

The work around the call

Robin only says what a person has approved. Every call is checked afterward, and every caller is asked how it went.

Why this exists

Robin needs good material to work from, and someone needs to check her afterward. Three systems handle that. The Knowledge Factory controls what she may say. The Grader checks whether each answer was right. The Quality Survey asks the caller whether it worked.

The three pieces

Before the call

The Knowledge Factory

The only way what Robin says can change.

  • Reads a plan document and pulls out pairs of questions and answers.
  • Finds the sentence behind each answer, and flags any answer with none.
  • Blocks anything holding personal information.
  • A person approves. The approved articles then upload into Robin.

After the call

The Grader

Checks whether each answer was right.

  • Takes every claim Robin made on the call.
  • Finds the sentence in the documents she used that supports it.
  • Marks the claims that no sentence supports.
  • Skips balances and loan figures, which come from a live lookup.

At the end of the call

The Quality Survey

Asks the caller whether it worked.

  • Would you do it this way again, or hold for a person?
  • How likely are you to recommend this?
  • How natural did the voice sound?
  • Anything else you want to tell us.

Who does what

The system gathers

  • Every call, every claim, and the sentence behind it.
  • A list of the questions nobody has answered yet.
  • The calls that scored badly, with the reasons.
  • What callers said about the experience.

A person decides

  • What the plan documents actually mean.
  • Whether the wording is right, and whether to publish it.
  • Whether a bad score was fair.
  • What gets written next.

What you can see

All of it lands on one page, grouped by question and by topic. It shows what callers asked, how each answer scored, and what callers said afterward. Clicking any figure opens the calls behind it.
