Does INTRUST match what I put into my 401(k)?

Yes. INTRUST matches a dollar for every dollar you contribute, up to six percent of your pay, and
that match is one hundred percent yours right away. There is no waiting period on it.

So if you contribute six percent of your pay, INTRUST puts in another six percent. If you contribute
less than six percent, you still get a dollar-for-dollar match on whatever you do put in.

The separate three percent contribution
There is also a three percent contribution INTRUST makes on its own to eligible employees who are
not active participants in the INTRUST Financial Corporation Employees' Retirement Plan. You do not
have to contribute anything yourself to receive it. Unlike the match, this one does follow a vesting
schedule, and you become fully vested in it after three years of service.

What is yours from day one
Your own contributions, any money you rolled in from a former employer's plan, and the safe harbor
match are all one hundred percent vested immediately.

the three percent contribution; the definition of compensation the plan uses to calculate the match.
