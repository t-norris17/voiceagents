What Robin is, and how she works

A voice agent that answers the plan's phone line, proves who is calling, and hands off to a person when she cannot finish.

Robin is not one system. She is an agent configured at ElevenLabs, a small amount of our own code, a database of member records, and a set of surfaces we watch her through. Four outside companies are involved and no others: Twilio provides the number, ElevenLabs runs her, Vercel hosts our code, Supabase holds the records. That is the whole list of things that have to be working for a caller to get an answer.

How a call works

  1. She asks what you need. Robin answers and asks what the caller needs before asking who they are. A name spoken aloud is a greeting, not an identity.
  2. She proves who is calling. Member ID and date of birth, checked against our records. From that point she carries a meaningless reference code rather than the person's details, and she never asks for a Social Security number, user ID, password or PIN.
  3. She confirms the plan. She reads the plan name back and waits for a yes. If it is wrong she stops and offers to connect them rather than answering from the wrong rules.
  4. She answers from her binder. Six plan documents that sit with her rather than with us, so a plan answer costs no time on the call. Where they are silent she says she is not certain and offers a person.
  5. She looks up their own numbers, but only when the question needs them. She cannot be interrupted during a lookup, after a caller's "okay" once cancelled one and left her answering with no account data.
  6. She connects, then records. Past what she can do she offers to put them through. When the call ends, its transcript, topic, outcome and verification result write themselves to the record.

What she is made of

Her rules
What must be true on every call: check who is calling first, never say a Social Security number, offer a transfer rather than a number to dial. These are instructions she follows, not locks in the software, which is why the identity result is recorded on every call and a separate review checks what she said.
Four playbooks
Topic guides for account recovery, plan questions, loans and the website, switched on by what the caller says.
Her binder
Six plan documents. She answers from those only, and is not allowed to fill a gap herself.
Two lookups
Is this really them, and what are their own figures. The only questions she can ask our systems.
Voice and turn-taking
A generated voice that adjusts how a line lands. She starts replying while the caller is still talking, and makes a short sound past three seconds rather than leaving silence.
Language
English, and Spanish with its own greeting, switched when the caller switches.
Guardrails
Topic-focus and prompt-injection protection on. Every call recorded, transcribed and classified.
