Robin Overview

A conversational voice agent that answers the plan's phone line.

Robin is built using ElevenLabs. She can confirm who is calling, answer plan questions from an approved set of documents, read a verified caller their own balance and loan details, put someone through to a person when a question goes beyond her, and hold the call in English or Spanish. Everything she says about the plan comes from documents a person reviewed and published.

How a call works

  1. She asks what you need. Robin answers and asks what the caller needs before asking who they are. A name spoken aloud is a greeting, not an identity.
  2. She proves who is calling. Member ID and date of birth, checked against our records. From then she carries a meaningless reference code, not the person's details, and never asks for a Social Security number, user ID, password or PIN.
  3. She confirms the plan. She reads the plan name back and waits for a yes. If it is wrong she stops and offers to connect them rather than answering from the wrong rules.
  4. She answers from her binder. The plan documents sit with her rather than with us, so a plan answer costs no time on the call. Where they are silent she says she is not certain and offers a person.
  5. She looks up their own numbers, but only when the question needs them. She cannot be interrupted during a lookup, after a caller's "okay" once cancelled one and left her answering with no account data.
  6. She connects, then records. Past what she can do she offers to put them through. When the call ends, its transcript, topic, outcome and verification result write themselves to the record.

What she is made of

Her rules
What must be true on every call: check who is calling first, never say a Social Security number, offer a transfer rather than a number to dial. These are instructions she follows, not locks in the software, which is why every call's identity result is recorded and separately reviewed.
Her playbooks
Topic guides, one per subject area, switched on by what the caller says.
Her binder
The plan documents she is allowed to answer from. She answers from those only, and may not fill a gap herself.
Two lookups
Is this really them, and what are their own figures. The only questions she can ask our systems.
Voice and turn-taking
A generated voice that adjusts how a line lands, in English or Spanish. She starts replying while the caller is still talking, and makes a short sound past three seconds rather than leaving silence.
Guardrails
Topic-focus and prompt-injection protection on. Every call recorded, transcribed and classified.
