Robin Overview

A conversational voice agent that provides helpful information and assistance 24/7.

Robin is built using ElevenLabs. She can confirm who is calling, answer plan questions from an approved set of documents, read a verified caller their own balance and loan details, put someone through to a person when a question goes beyond her, and hold the call in English or Spanish. Everything she says about the plan comes from documents a person reviewed and published.

How a call works

Greeting
Robin greets the caller and gathers their name and their reason for calling.
Verification
Robin asks for verification information and confirms it against an identity database.
Answering
Once verified, Robin begins answering their question.
Conversation
The call continues back and forth, with Robin answering questions and providing information, until the caller is satisfied or needs to be transferred to a person.
Recording
Once the call has ended, Robin records the results and the data she collected to a database for quality assurance review.

What she is made of

Rules
Robin must verify each caller, transfer when she cannot provide an answer or perform an action, and avoid any topic outside of her approved list.
Procedures
Each subject Robin handles has its own procedure. What the caller asks decides which one she uses.
Knowledge Base
Robin answers only from an approved set of documents. Where they do not cover a question, she says so rather than guessing.
Tools
Robin possesses a toolbox that allows her to query databases when providing information such as loan status and balances.
Context-aware speech
Robin is built to be context-aware. She will adjust her tone, pace, and conversation based on the caller to best serve them.
Guardrails
Robin is equipped with guardrails that protect her both from bad actors and from drifting into non-compliant territory. These either end the conversation immediately or redirect her with a kind message.
