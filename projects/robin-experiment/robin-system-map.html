How it fits together

Robin takes the calls. Four other pieces sit around her.

The four are not parts of Robin. One decides what she knows, one checks that knowledge without placing a call, and two watch what happens on every call she takes. Each has its own page.

What goes in

Knowledge Factory
Turns a plan document into answers Robin can give out loud, checks every claim against the original, and waits for a person to approve it.
Question Tester
Ask a question in a browser and see what her published knowledge answers, without placing a call or tying up a tester.

What comes out

Accuracy
Checks each answer against the documents she was actually shown on that call, claim by claim, with the sentence behind each one.
Quality
Four questions at the end of a call, asking whether the caller would rather handle it this way again or hold for a person.
